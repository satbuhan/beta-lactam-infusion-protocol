Beta-Lactam Infusion Strategy in Sepsis and Septic Shock

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24201599

Educational / academic reference tool, not a substitute for institutional protocol or clinical judgement at the bedside. This algorithm applies to critically ill adults with sepsis or septic shock receiving piperacillin-tazobactam or meropenem; it should not be extrapolated to ward-based or non-critically ill populations, nor to patients receiving renal replacement therapy or extracorporeal membrane oxygenation. This tool's reference list is reproduced directly from the source manuscript's own bibliography and was not independently re-verified against primary sources for this build — see the References section and METHODOLOGY.md for details.
